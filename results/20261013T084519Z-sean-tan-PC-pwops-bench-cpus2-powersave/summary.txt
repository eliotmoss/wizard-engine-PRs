Each operation is its own committed transaction except commit-empty (production commits nothing for
it: no boundary) and timer (an empty rdtsc window, the floor under every figure). mount-* opens a
cleanly closed region; recover-k opens a 1 MiB region whose log holds one committed, unapplied
transaction of k entries, so replay-k = recover-k - mount-1mib. lines counts writeback requests
(pmem and direct; 0 where the backend has none). pmem-none drops CLWB and keeps SFENCE: an
approximation of eADR timing, never durable. A difference is real only outside both spreads.
