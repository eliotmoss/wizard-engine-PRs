# same-boot = the reopen in the same process: recovery, marker visible
# log x/yz = buffered and O_DIRECT copies of the log chunk equal (x), nonce in buffered (y) and direct (z)
# after-drop = fault-verify after umount + mount: recovery, visible, verdict
# fs = rw or ro after the reopen (ro: a journal failure, not the case under test)
