---- nvme1n1 requests: complete pwbench processes and equal-length idle windows
Linux rwbs: leading F = PREFLUSH or standalone FLUSH; F after the operation = FUA.
F-any is the union, not the sum; a request may carry both flags.
Traces include setup/close, warm-up, root journal commits (commit=5), desktop IO and /boot/efi IO.
Counts are grouped by comm for context, never filtered: jbd2/kworker can issue benchmark IO.
Ratios use the measured persist boundaries count reported by pwbench (one fdatasync each).
That denominator excludes setup/close and warm-up, which remain in the whole-process numerator.
