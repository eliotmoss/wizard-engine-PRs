Columns: median, p90 and mean of each run's per-operation rdtsc times; spread = (max - min) / median
of the per-run medians; the phases are PWBTree's onPhase marks, means per operation; persist =
alloc + write + publish + free; share = persist / mean, the persistence phases over the whole
operation's mean time (rdtsc around each put or get), as pwsieve-bench.sh's share is over a
whole step's wall time; what the marks leave out counts against it. A mutation at height h
with k new nodes makes k + h + 1 boundaries: k allocations, one fence over the new nodes, the
publish (root swap and first free) and h - 1 further frees. Inserts include the load's first
inserts, made while the root is still a leaf. pmem-none drops CLWB and keeps SFENCE: an
approximation of eADR timing, never durable. A difference is real only outside both spreads.
